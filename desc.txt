Curiosite is an online learning platform where instructors create and sell courses, and students learn at their own pace. The backend powers everything behind the scenes: user accounts and secure sign-in, student and instructor profiles with connections between people, course catalogs with videos, articles, quizzes, and assignments, progress tracking, and completion certificates. Students can fund a wallet, buy individual courses, or subscribe to a plan, while a leaderboard and experience points keep learning engaging. A built-in messaging system lets learners and instructors chat in real time, including group conversations for each course. An AI study assistant answers questions about lessons and can read them aloud. Notifications keep everyone informed, and administrators get tools to review instructors, manage courses, and monitor activity. Instructors and students can follow each other's posts, like and comment, and build a learning community around shared interests. Everything runs securely on the cloud, ready for use on any device.
